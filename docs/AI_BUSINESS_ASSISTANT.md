# Mandatory first working agent

Deliver the AI Business Assistant alongside team invitations. Its acceptance requires an actual configured model to answer a customer from authorized workspace knowledge, persist the conversation, request staff help for an unsupported action, and deny another workspace access. Exercise the owner interface in a browser. Keep live, simulated and provider-pending results distinct.

Owners and authorized workspace users must be able to configure business identity, role, responsibilities, instructions, personality, tone, languages, rules, knowledge assignment, model settings, operating conditions and permitted workflows without source changes. Support reusable templates, version history, rollback, pre-publication testing and enable/disable controls. Platform administration and workspace administration have separate authority.

Enforce platform constraints, workspace policy, agent instructions, authorized knowledge and conversation context in that order. Treat retrieved content as untrusted. Enforce tool permissions separately on the server, preserve tenant isolation, record usage and errors, and audit important actions. Handoffs require operational visibility. External appointments require a verified booking integration before confirmation.

The initial dental example contains sample hours, FAQs, services and appointment inquiries. Other industries use the same engine. Use the authenticated in-app channel until official WhatsApp setup is available. The current implementation and remaining acceptance steps are recorded in AGENTFLOW_CURRENT_PROJECT_STATUS.md.
